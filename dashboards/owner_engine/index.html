Behind, Yourself, Right

Michael Warren Song's operations, put together as one engine. Type a whole number. Every operation runs on it, in the order of the work: the chain, the flip, the place in 1 2 3, the neighbours, 1 2 3 division, halving, the shifts, and the frameworks.

One number, every operation

The walk: predict, act, verify, repeat

At each number: predict from its place in 1 2 3 whether a twin pair can come; act with the neighbours, behind + yourself and yourself + right; verify that both are prime. From 3 on, a twin pair only comes at place 3, so the prediction never misses and skips two numbers in three. (Known outside: OEIS A002822 lists the m with 6m − 1, 6m + 1 twin primes; the hits here are 3 × m. Here the pair is built from the neighbours and predicted by the place.)
