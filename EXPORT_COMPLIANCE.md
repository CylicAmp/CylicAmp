# Export Compliance Notes – Encryption Algorithms

This repository contains a conceptual post-quantum algorithm prototype (pqc_third_option). Strong encryption is subject to U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR).

## Key Requirements
- The algorithm is a research prototype only and provides no real security.
- It remains private and is not exported until proper classification, review, and any required licenses or exceptions are obtained.
- Any plan to share across borders, publish, or distribute must first be reviewed for EAR compliance.

## Recommended Steps
1. Keep repository strictly private.
2. Consult a Texas IP/export-control attorney before any cross-border discussion or release.
3. Document all secrecy measures in TRADE_SECRET_LOG.md and LICENSE.

This note ensures the third-option algorithm prototype is developed and protected in full compliance with U.S. law.
