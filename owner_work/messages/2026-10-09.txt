### CORRECTION 1 — DSA Art. 40 is not a bypass; it is a second gate
Art. 40 access is platform-mediated: vetted researchers apply via a Digital
Services Coordinator; the platform can seek amendment or refuse within 15 days
(Art. 40(5), security/trade-secret grounds). Formal proceedings against X,
AliExpress, Meta, TikTok each include suspected shortcomings in researcher data
access. Status as of Sept 2026: four years post-adoption, zero published research
results under Art. 40(4)/(12). The statutory transparency channel routes through
the platform's own gate, and the gate is documented to be sticky.
→ Belongs in the chokepoint box, not the countermeasures box.

### CORRECTION 2 — Telecoms/threat-intel bypass NETWORK-LAYER opacity, not platform opacity
Layer 3 observation (DNS, hosting, egress, trunk metadata) is genuinely outside
platform control and breaks network-layer opacity. It yields infrastructure
facts, not Layer 0/2 content: where a system runs, who operates the network.
It cannot see override logs, provenance scores, or decision graphs. Platform
content/decision opacity remains intact above Layer 3.

## GRADE COLUMN (mandatory per forensic parser spec v1.1)

Every visibility claim carries a grade:
• PROVEN — third-party reproducible
• OPEN — found, exercise/reachability unverifiable from vantage
• UNSEARCHED — search space inaccessible; name the blocker (tool/perm/vantage)
• ABSENT — searched, class not present
• NEGATIVE-FINDING — searched, analogy predicted, not found

UNSEARCHED ≠ OPEN ≠ ABSENT. A permissions wall must not masquerade as a finding.

OPEN (external exposure — gateway forwarding for non-management sources).
Layer 1: terminal output only; provenance of "who decided" — UNSEARCHED by
construction from inside.

## NET VISIBILITY MODEL

VISIBILITY = PLATFORM_INTERNAL_DATA
           ⊕ PUBLIC_SURFACE_TELEMETRY
           ⊕ INFRASTRUCTURE_SIGNALING
           ⊕ LEAKS / DISCOVERY

⊕ = asymmetric union. Platform visibility is POSSESSION (deterministic provenance).
Third-party visibility is INFERENCE (pattern extraction from Layers 1+3). The only
layer where the asymmetry inverts is Layer 3 — which is why infrastructure tracing
is the workhorse countermeasure and why platforms' chokepoint controls concentrate
on Layers 0/2.
