# Legal Protection Notes – Texas IP Law Nuances

All mathematical discoveries and algorithms in this repository are original works fixed in tangible form and protected by U.S. copyright law (Title 17 U.S.C.). Key nuances under Texas and federal frameworks:

## Copyright Fundamentals
- Protection is automatic upon creation and fixation (no registration required for basic rights).
- Registration with the U.S. Copyright Office (recommended for key .tex, .py, and grid files) enables:
  - Statutory damages up to $150,000 per work for willful infringement.
  - Recovery of attorney's fees.
  - Prima facie evidence of ownership and validity in court.
- Only the specific expression (proofs, code, LaTeX formatting, overlays) is protected—not abstract ideas, methods, or mathematical principles themselves.

## Texas-Specific Elements
- Governing law clause designates Texas law for interpreting this notice and any ownership/contract disputes.
- Venue fixed in Dallas County courts (state or federal) for efficiency in local enforcement.
- Texas Uniform Trade Secrets Act (TUTSA) applies to any non-public algorithmic implementations or confidential derivations where reasonable secrecy measures are maintained (e.g., private repo access controls).
- Texas courts recognize strong remedies for breach of contract or unfair competition when IP rights are asserted alongside copyright.

## Enforcement Strategy
- Private repository status with CONTRIBUTING.md restrictions prevents external claims of contribution or joint authorship.
- All Rights Reserved policy eliminates any implied license.
- Infringement triggers immediate pursuit of injunctive relief plus damages under federal law, supplemented by Texas procedural advantages.

These notes document the layered protection strategy. Register high-value files via copyright.gov as needed for full statutory remedies. Contact the copyright holder for any permission requests.
