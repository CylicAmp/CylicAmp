\section{Statement}

Let $\Sigma\phi(n) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \phi(k)$ denote the cumulative sum of Euler's totient function.

At $n=34$, $\Sigma\phi(34) = 360$.

This value satisfies:
\[
360 \equiv 36 \pmod{81}
\]

The position 36 fits the coordinate pair $(a,b) = (3,6)$ or $(6,3)$ where $a+b=9$ (digital root 9 container).

On the 9×9 cyclic grid, 360 marks the milestone positions with digital root 9. The overlay is:

\]

Additionally, $360 \equiv 27 \pmod{37}$ where $27 = 3^3$ completes the circle.

The sequence continues to the algebraic container at 432 with gap 72 ($=8\times9$).
