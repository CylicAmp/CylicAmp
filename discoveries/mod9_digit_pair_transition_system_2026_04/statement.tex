\section{Statement}

The mod-9 digit-pair transition system governs all adjacency and swap transitions over the digit-pair lattice $[11, 99]$ in three layers:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Adjacency Lattice}: $S \to S+1$ (linear) or $(d_1,d_2) \to (d_2,d_1)$ (mirror swap)
\item \textbf{Swap Symmetry}: Every pair $(d_1,d_2)$ has mirror $(d_2,d_1)$; both share the same digital root
\item \textbf{Mod-9 Collapse}: All transitions preserve the digital root under mod-9 reduction
\end{enumerate}
