# Mersenne Exponents and the 7-4-9 Triad

Analysis of known Mersenne prime exponents and their digital root alignment with the 7-4-9 triad.

## Mersenne Exponents Checked
[2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 17, 19, 31, 61, 89, 107, 127]

## Results
- p=7: DR=7 ✓
- p=13: DR=4 ✓
- p=31: DR=4 ✓
- p=61: DR=7 ✓
- All others: outside {4,7,9}
