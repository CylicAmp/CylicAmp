\section{Statement}

Among the known Mersenne prime exponents $p \in \{2,3,5,7,13,17,19,31,61,89,107,127\}$, four have digital roots in the 7-4-9 triad $\{4,7,9\}$:

\[
p = 7 \Rightarrow \mathrm{DR}=7, \quad
p = 13 \Rightarrow \mathrm{DR}=4, \quad
p = 31 \Rightarrow \mathrm{DR}=4, \quad
p = 61 \Rightarrow \mathrm{DR}=7.
\]

This 4-of-12 alignment grounds the Mersenne exponent sequence within the 7-4-9 triad system.
