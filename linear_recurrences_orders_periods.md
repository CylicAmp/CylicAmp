### Linear recurrences over finite fields: orders, periods, and primary decomposition

## 1. Basic definitions and terminology

Let \(\mathbb F_q\) be a finite field of characteristic \(p\), and consider a linear recurrence of order \(k\):
\[
u_n=c_1u_{n-1}+\cdots+c_ku_{n-k},
\qquad c_i\in\mathbb F_q.
\]
